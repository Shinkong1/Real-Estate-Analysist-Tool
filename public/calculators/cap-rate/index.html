Common questions

What is a good cap rate?

It depends on the market and property. In 2025 and 2026 most U.S. apartment deals traded between about 5% and 8%. Higher cap rates mean more income per dollar but often come with more risk, older buildings or weaker locations.

Does cap rate include the mortgage?

No. Cap rate uses NOI before debt payments, so it measures the property itself. Use cash-on-cash return or DSCR to see how financing changes the picture.

What counts as operating expenses?

Property taxes, insurance, repairs and maintenance, management, utilities the owner pays, payroll, landscaping, trash and a reserve for replacements. Not included: mortgage payments, depreciation and major renovations.
