Common questions

What DSCR do lenders require?

Most commercial and DSCR rental lenders want 1.20x to 1.25x. Some DSCR loan programs go down to 1.0x at a higher rate.

What if my DSCR is too low?

Borrow less (bigger down payment), negotiate a lower price, find a lower rate or longer amortization, or raise NOI before refinancing.

Does DSCR use gross rent?

No. It uses net operating income: rent minus vacancy and operating expenses, before the mortgage.
