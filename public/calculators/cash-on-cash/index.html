Common questions

What is a good cash-on-cash return?

Many investors look for 8 to 12% on buy-and-hold rentals. Lower can still make sense if you expect strong appreciation or rent growth; higher usually means more work or risk.

How is it different from ROI?

Cash-on-cash only counts the cash flow you receive each year. Total return also includes loan paydown, appreciation and tax benefits.

Should I include repairs in cash invested?

Yes. Any cash you put in to buy and stabilize the property belongs in the cash invested figure.
