Common questions

What is the 1% rule?

A quick screen: monthly rent should be at least 1% of the purchase price. It is a rough filter only; taxes, insurance and condition matter more.

How much should I budget for repairs and reserves?

A common rule of thumb is 5 to 10% of rent for repairs and another 5 to 10% for big-ticket replacements such as roofs, HVAC and appliances, more for older buildings.

Why is my cash flow negative?

Usually the price is high relative to rent, the interest rate is high, or taxes and insurance are heavy. Try a bigger down payment or a lower offer.
