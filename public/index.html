How it works

From listing link to a go or no-go in three steps

Paste or share the link

Share a listing straight from your browser or paste the link. If a site blocks readers, add a screenshot or the offering memorandum instead.

Review what it found

Price, units, rents, expenses and taxes are pulled out for you to check. Fix anything that looks off before it flows into the tools.

Underwrite the deal

Every tool fills in at once: cash flow, valuation, five-year projection, financing options, due diligence and tax angles. Save deals and compare them.
